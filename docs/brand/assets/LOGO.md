## Birincil Prompt (uzun versiyon)

Create an award-winning icon-only logo for a Startup Intelligence Platform called Huginn & Muninn.

Design a bold geometric symbol built from two angular structures connected by a diagonal pathway.

The symbol should subtly form an abstract "M" shape while remaining highly recognizable as an independent icon.

At the top of the diagonal connection place a single circular node representing:

- startups
- innovation signals
- founders
- opportunities
- investments

The icon should communicate:

- startup intelligence
- business discovery
- venture capital ecosystems
- company relationships
- innovation mapping
- strategic insight

Visual Style:

- premium B2B SaaS
- modern technology company
- geometric precision
- clean vector design
- strong silhouette
- app icon ready
- favicon ready
- Scandinavian minimalism

Composition:

- symmetrical balance
- sharp architectural forms
- meaningful negative space
- upward momentum
- dynamic but simple

Color Palette:

- deep navy
- electric blue
- vibrant indigo
- subtle cyan accents

Background:

- transparent

Avoid:

- birds
- ravens
- wings
- shields
- globes
- magnifying glasses
- maps
- cybersecurity imagery
- hacker aesthetics
- mascots
- illustrations

Brand Inspiration:

Stripe
Linear
Vercel
PitchBook
Crunchbase
Palantir

The final icon should feel like a billion-dollar startup intelligence platform used by founders, investors and market researchers.

## Alternatif Prompt (tek satir)

Minimal geometric logo icon, abstract M symbol made of two triangular structures connected by a diagonal line ending in a circular node, startup intelligence SaaS, venture capital technology platform, premium blue to violet gradient, clean vector, transparent background, strong silhouette, modern fintech aesthetic, icon only, no text, no empty space, brand identity quality.
