# Huginn & Muninn

## Category

Startup Intelligence Platform

## Positioning

Huginn & Muninn is a business intelligence platform that helps users discover startups, founders, companies, investors, market opportunities and innovation ecosystems.

The platform combines company discovery, startup intelligence, commercial research and ecosystem mapping into a single SaaS experience.

## Mission

Help builders, investors, founders and analysts discover opportunities earlier through better business intelligence.

## Vision

Map the global innovation economy.

# Logo Meaning

The logo represents the journey from raw data to actionable intelligence.

Elements:

1. Upper node
   - Startup
   - Opportunity
   - Innovation signal
   - Market event

2. Diagonal connection
   - Discovery
   - Intelligence flow
   - Knowledge transfer

3. Left structure
   - Data collection
   - Research
   - Intelligence gathering

4. Right structure
   - Analysis
   - Understanding
   - Strategic advantage

5. Negative space
   - Hidden insights
   - Unseen connections
   - Opportunity pathways

Overall interpretation:

A modern intelligence engine that transforms information into opportunity.
